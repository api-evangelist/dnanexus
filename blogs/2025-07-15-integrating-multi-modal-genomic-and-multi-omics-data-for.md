The Importance of Multi-Modal Data Integration in Precision Medicine Precision medicine aims to tailor medical treatment to the individual characteristics of each patient or subpopulations of patients. One of the key enablers of precision medicine approaches is the integration of multi-modal omics data. By combining diverse datasets, such as genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and environmental data, researchers, clinicians, and other precision health stakeholders gain a more comprehensive understanding of the factors influencing health and disease.
